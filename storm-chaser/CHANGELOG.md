# Changelog

## v2 — 30 July 2026

First revision after playtesting v1 with a 3 yr 9 mth old.

### Changed

- **All design rationale moved off the play screen into the report.** During play the
  screen now shows the child's question plus one short line for the adult. Facilitators
  are supervising a small child; they cannot read paragraphs mid-item.
- **Every quantity randomised per run.** v1 was fixed, so a second run measured recall
  rather than skill. Counting sets, flash counts, comparison pairs, addends, splits,
  pattern objects, shapes and rotations are all generated at run time.
- **Fine motor demand removed.** No item now depends on a young child's mouse or tap
  precision. Where precision was the barrier, the adult records the observation instead.
- **Re-running a section replaces its results** rather than merging with the previous
  run. Added a run counter and a "Clear all results" button.

### Fixed

- **Foundation 1.2** — replaced tap-each-car with adult observation of the child counting
  by pointing. The tapping was never the construct, and on a web page nothing indicated
  the cars were targets.
- **Foundation 2.1** — the tornado now visibly carries a car off screen. Previously the
  car faded to 28% opacity, which a 3-year-old reads as "a faded car", not "gone". The
  subtraction was never presented.
- **Foundation 2.2** — the full set is now shown and counted before any of it is hidden.
- **All arithmetic items** now answer with pictures of cars, not written numerals.
  Numeral answers silently required numeral recognition — a separate foundation,
  measured on its own in 1.5.
- **Foundation 1.6** — asks "fewer" and then "more" on the same picture, distinguishing
  four outcomes. Two of them are labelled *vocabulary, not maths*.

### Split

- **Foundation 4.1** separated into non-verbal shape matching (Spatial thinking) and
  shape naming (Math language). Naming shapes is vocabulary; recognising a rotated square
  as a square is geometry. A child with no shape words can now score at the later band.

### Redesigned

- **Foundation 4.6** — "turn the car until it fits" became "which car will fit?".
  Rotating until something fits is the trial-and-error strategy that indicates the child
  is *not* rotating mentally, so scoring it rewarded the wrong thing. It also asked for
  motor control a 3-year-old does not have.

### Report

- Every foundation now carries: what it measures · why it is in the check · what the
  child did · what that means for this band · what to try next.
- Foundations grouped into Number sense · Patterns & sorting · Spatial thinking ·
  Math language, so a vocabulary gap cannot be misread as a number-sense gap.
